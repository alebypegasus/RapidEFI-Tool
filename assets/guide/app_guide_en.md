# RapidEFI User Guide

Welcome to **RapidEFI**, a comprehensive tool designed to streamline the process of building and managing EFI configurations for macOS systems. This guide will walk you through the core features of the application.

## 1. Hardware Info & ACPI Extraction
The Home page allows you to view your system's hardware configuration.
- **Dump ACPI:** Automatically extract your system's native DSDT and SSDT files for further patching.
- **Export Report:** Save a detailed hardware report alongside your ACPI tables.

## 2. Configure EFI
RapidEFI provides a simplified way to configure your EFI:
- **Platform Selection:** Choose your target architecture (Intel or AMD) and generation.
- **Kexts & Drivers:** Select necessary network, audio, Bluetooth, and UEFI drivers.
- **Build EFI:** Once configured, RapidEFI compiles the EFI folder for you to place in your ESP (EFI System Partition).

## 3. SSDT Tools
Navigate to **Tools & Guides > Custom SSDT** to manage ACPI patches:
- Automatically determine required SSDTs based on your hardware.
- Easily create custom patches like `SSDT-HPET`, `SSDT-PNLF`, and GPU Spoofing.
- Merge the generated SSDT files and configuration directly into your existing `config.plist`.

## 4. OCLP Patches
For legacy hardware support in newer macOS versions:
- Apply Wi-Fi and GPU patches using the integrated OCLP logic.
- Ensure your system meets the requirements listed in the respective OCLP section before patching.

## 5. History & Settings
- **History:** Keep track of your previously generated EFIs and logs.
- **Settings:** Customize the application appearance (Dark/Light Mode), change the display font, and configure log limits.

---
*Tip: Always keep a backup of your working EFI folder before applying new changes generated by RapidEFI.*
